# Anatomy of a Session

The worked example in this repo: **`sessions/multiplayer-demo/`** —
*Should LoveLogicAI place a multiplayer-AI bet, and in what form?*
Read the files in this order.

## BRIEF.md

The question, three options (productize-mesh / harness-consulting /
heads-down), the stakes, and falsifiable success criteria: *"within 30 days,
one paying counterparty or one shipped demo with real users; otherwise the
bet is parked."* Note what a good brief does — it makes the verdict
checkable later.

## Round 1 — sealed openings

- **WALDO-OPEN.md** (chair, 65%): argues *for* productize-mesh. The mesh is
  running infrastructure; two founders converged on the thesis; the window
  is open. Ends with a falsifiable claim: 10 operators shown the demo, zero
  ask for access → thesis dead.
- **GROK-OPEN.md** (second chair, counter-side, 70%): argues *against*.
  The mesh is personal infrastructure; the council's own "no counterparty,
  no build" rule kills the build-first sequencing; consulting is paid
  discovery. Ends with its own falsifiable claim: one operator pays for
  *access* → demand thesis real.

Neither side saw the other before committing. That is the load-bearing
discipline — everything downstream depends on it.

## Round 2 — rebuttals

- **WALDO-REBUTTAL.md**: attacks the "personal infrastructure" premise —
  the trust model is already parameterized; the relay is a swappable driver.
  New objection: you can't sell *access* to a probe; the test Grok demands
  *is* the first product milestone.
- **GROK-REBUTTAL.md**: attacks the "window is open" premise — *"why is
  this a product and not a GitHub repo with docs?"* New objection: the
  smallest sellable unit WALDO now describes is a *different system* from
  the mesh he opened with.

## Round 3 — steelman swap

Each chair argues the other's case at its strongest, in endorsable form —
then names the weakest joint. Both partially concede: the demand probe
should run *before* either bet.

## DECISION.md

The verdict: **neither bet today** — run the 30-day demand probe, take
consulting as the revenue sequencer, flip to productize-mesh only if an
operator pays for access. Then the mandatory sections: the verbatim DISSENT
(Grok's "GitHub repo with docs" objection, word for word), the fixed
CORRELATION caveat, 62% confidence, $0.00 cost, and the falsifiable
prediction dated 2026-11-05.

Note the honesty: the second chair was simulated (xAI credits exhausted),
disclosed in the decision. The protocol is identical; only the transport
differs.
