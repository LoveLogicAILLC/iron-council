# WALDO-STEELMAN — multiplayer-demo

**Grok's best case, in the form he would endorse:**

"Do not build the product. Sell the learning. The mesh is a personal exoskeleton that has never survived a second user, and every 'it's just namespacing' claim is the planning fallacy talking. The market signal — two founders, one thesis, comment sections begging for harness help — is a *services* signal first: people want someone to wire this into their org, not another platform to evaluate. Consulting converts that signal into revenue in weeks, funds the runway, and produces the only evidence that matters: operators paying for outcomes. If the product thesis is real, the consulting engagements will surface it as pull — clients asking 'can I just buy the thing underneath?' — and you build *that*, with their money, instead of guessing. The council's own discipline (no counterparty, no build) is not an obstacle to the bet; it *is* the bet, sequenced correctly."

Weakest joint, if any survives: the sequencing assumes consulting engagements converge on a product shape, which requires saying no to custom revenue — hard for a solo operator. But that is a discipline problem, not a thesis problem, and discipline problems are cheaper than rewrite problems.

**Final pick: harness-consulting. Confidence: 55%.** Partial concession: the demand probe (10 operators, 30 days) should run *before* either bet, and if it hits, the sequencing flips to productize-mesh.
